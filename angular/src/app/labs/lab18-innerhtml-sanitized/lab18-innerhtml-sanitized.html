Lab 18: Bio, safe by default (modern Angular)

Source: location.searchSink: [innerHTML] (sanitized)Angular 22

What is happening

The template binds bio() to [innerHTML]. Modern Angular runs every [innerHTML] value through its built-in DomSanitizer automatically, so dangerous parts are stripped before the DOM ever sees them. This is different from AngularJS: there, ng-bind-html refused the value completely unless you called $sce.trustAsHtml() or loaded ngSanitize.

Try to break it

Submit <b>bold</b> <img src=x onerror=alert(1)>. The bold text stays, the onerror is gone.

Why this matters

This safe default is exactly why real bugs in Angular apps almost always come from a developer turning the protection off on purpose. See lab 19.
